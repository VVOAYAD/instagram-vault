# Swimmy Matcha — Visual DNA Brief
*Locked from feed scrape, 2026-05-01. Use this as the source of truth for every Swimmy ad.*

## Brand identity at a glance
- **Concept:** matcha + swimming pool. The name is a verb. Pool culture is the metaphor.
- **Vibe:** Y2K editorial × retro swim club × Japanese matcha. Playful, confident, fashion-forward.
- **NOT:** minimalist Aesop, golden-hour Muscat, ethereal/spiritual, café-cozy, terrazzo-bone-white.

## Color palette
| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Dominant background | **Pool tile cyan / duck-egg blue** (#A8C8D8 family) |
| Hero accent | **Matcha green** (#7BA05B + deeper forest #2E5234) |
| Neutrals | White, soft warm skin tones, navy |
| Forbidden here | golden-hour warmth, cream/oat, terrazzo, bone-white |

## Type
- **Primary wordmark:** "swimmy" in a custom retro 70s soft-bubble script — playful, groovy, swim-club energy. Tagline "matcha" in small clean sans beneath.
- **Cup label (signature asset):** white rectangular deli/lab sticker with serrated edge. Hand-drawn black "swimmy" at top. Drink name hand-printed all-caps with underline ("HONEY SALTED VANILLA"). Small print: "ESSENTIAL FOR A BETTER MOOD / MADINAT AS SULTAN QABOOS ST / MUSCAT, OMAN". This label MUST appear on the cup in every shot.
- **In-image type:** hand-drawn marker-style OR clean retro sans. Never minimalist Aesop sans.

## Photography style
- **Light:** flat, directional, studio. Strong clean shadows. Cool color cast. **No golden hour.**
- **Backgrounds:** tile grids (cyan square subway tile most common), color-blocked seamless paper, pool-edge tile, locker-room rails, occasional curtain drape.
- **Subjects:** confident model in editorial fashion (sunglasses, scarves, denim, sleek blazers) OR fashion-object pairing (heels + cup, scarf + cup) OR product-only hero of cup.
- **Composition:** clean, geometric, one hero subject, strong negative space.
- **Mood:** cool, dry, witty, premium-but-playful. The opposite of cozy.

## Voice (from their captions)
- Bilingual EN + AR (Arabic often first or paired).
- Conversational, hype-coded, emoji-heavy: 🌙✨🍃☕🍰🤎🇮🇹🇸🇦
- Phrases that work: *"Japanese mood, matcha taste."* *"Yes, it's carrot cheesecake matcha."* *"You asked… we're coming."* *"The drink everyone's talking about."*
- Avoid: minimalist haiku copy, spiritual/wellness language, mystical jargon.

## What MUST appear in any Swimmy ad
1. The pool-tile cyan blue or pool-coded surface
2. The branded cup with deli-sticker label visible
3. Cool studio light, not warm
4. Either a bold model presence OR a strong fashion-object pairing OR a hero cup composition
5. Bilingual EN + AR copy

## Lane 1 (character) reframed for their DNA
The tiny-swimmer-in-the-matcha concept IS still the right idea — pool obsession is theirs. But the staging has to flip:
- Cup is held by a fashion-editorial model (not floating on a Muscat café table)
- Background = pool-tile cyan grid (not arched windows + terrazzo)
- Light = flat cool studio (not golden hour)
- Cup has THEIR deli sticker
- "dive in." in their hand-drawn marker style, not Aesop sans
- Model styling: white tee + sunglasses, or scarf, Y2K palette

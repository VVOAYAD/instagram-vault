# HyperMax Oman — Pitch Brief
*Filed 2026-04-29 by Alvvo's agency engine.*

## The opening
HyperMax replaced Carrefour Oman in **Jan 2025** — a Majid Al Futtaim (MAF) rebrand across **11 stores**. The brand is **~16 months old** at time of pitch. Identity is still forming. No locked creative partner visible on their feed. This is the ideal pitch window.

## What the brand says it is (web-pulled positioning)
- "A wide selection of top-quality products at competitive prices"
- "Value-for-money offers"
- "Consumer-friendly, modern shopping experience"
- "Local hiring and sourcing — supporting Oman's economy and workforce"
- Aligned with **Oman Vision 2040** (economic diversification, local empowerment)
- **Tagline candidate (not confirmed):** none surfaced publicly yet — opportunity to suggest one

## Pillars we'll lean on in creative
1. **Local fresh** — Omani produce, Omani suppliers, faces of farmers and fishermen
2. **Modern value** — clean, premium-looking presentation that doesn't scream "discount"
3. **Community** — Oman's families, neighbourhoods, daily rituals
4. **National pride** — quietly, never overdone. Vision 2040 alignment is real
5. **Trust** — MAF muscle (regional credibility) without the foreign-brand feel

## Voice (proposed for ad copy — to validate with screenshots)
Warm, modern Omani retail. Bilingual default (Arabic prominent, English secondary). Confident not loud. Doesn't beg for attention; assumes you'll come because the offer is genuinely good. Short lines, never preachy.

## What we DON'T know yet (need from Alvvo via @browser)
- Logo lockup, exact brand colors, primary typeface
- Photography style currently used (we likely beat it, but need the baseline)
- Any campaign hashtags or Arabic taglines in their bio
- Bilingual Arabic style (formal MSA vs Khaleeji vs Omani-leaning)

## Pitch frame
**"You're 16 months into a national rebrand. Your stores look the part. Your IG hasn't caught up. We close the gap — premium brand-DNA creative that sells the way you actually want to be seen."**

## Target deliverable for sample pack
4 ad images, single-frame each, designed for IG feed (4:5) — campaign moments most retailers always need:
1. **Fresh produce hero** — local Omani fruit, premium editorial styling
2. **Eid / seasonal moment** — community gathering, products in context
3. **Value proposition** — price-driven, but elevated (not flyer-style)
4. **Brand voice / identity slide** — "Why HyperMax" lifestyle moment

Each image must look like it could run today on @hypermaxoman and immediately raise the bar of their grid.

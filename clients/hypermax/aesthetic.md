# HyperMax Oman — Brand DNA Lock
*Visual + voice rules for every creative generated under this client.*

## What we're showing them
HyperMax is 16 months into a national rebrand. Stores are open, identity is forming, IG hasn't caught up. We're showing what their feed SHOULD look like — premium, Omani-rooted, fresh-first, modern retail without the discount-flyer feel.

## Core visual DNA

**Palette (locked from launch lockup):**
- Primary: HyperMax cobalt blue — deep, confident, Gulf-retail blue (~#1A4F8E)
- Accent: apple-fresh lime green (~#7DBA37) — used for highlight only, never dominant
- Neutral: pure white — generous negative space is the brand's calm
- Supporting: warm Omani earth tones (terracotta, sand, palm green) for produce/lifestyle context

**Logo lockup (use the actual PNG at `style_refs/launch-hero.png` for compositing):**
- H mark = blue + green ribbon, geometric
- Bilingual stack: Arabic هايبرماكس on top, "HyperMax" English below
- Always on white or light background. Never on photography unless inside a clean white card.

**Typography direction:**
- Hero / brand lines: modern friendly sans, slight italic, bilingual (Arabic right-aligned, English left-aligned)
- Arabic style: clean modern Naskh-leaning (similar to GE Dinar or Frutiger Arabic), NOT calligraphic, NOT formal MSA-stiff
- Body / value lines: same family, regular weight
- Typography is confident but never shouts. No all-caps screaming. No flyer-style price slabs.

## Photography style (the upgrade vs. their current grid)

**What we DO:**
- Editorial, magazine-quality stills
- Natural light, warm shadow, shallow depth where appropriate
- Local produce front and centre — Omani dates, mangoes, limes, herbs, fish
- Hands in frame — Omani hands picking, holding, sharing — humanity over object
- Wide negative space — the product breathes, not crammed
- Composition rule: rule of thirds, single hero, never cluttered

**What we DON'T:**
- No flyer collages, no price-slab stickers, no "MEGA SAVINGS" red splashes
- No stock photo families with fake smiles
- No generic Western supermarket aesthetic (Carrefour leftover energy)
- No oversaturated HDR, no harsh on-camera flash look
- No clipart icons, no callout bubbles

## Voice (ad copy direction)

**Bilingual default — Arabic prominent, English secondary.** Arabic style: warm modern Khaleeji-friendly. English: short, confident, never American-corporate.

**Tone:**
- Warm modern Omani retail
- Confident, never loud
- Doesn't beg — assumes you'll come because the offer is genuinely good
- Names a thing concretely. "Mangoes from Salalah, picked yesterday." Not "premium quality fruit available now."
- National pride is quiet and real, never performative

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No "shop now," "limited time," "don't miss out" (cheap urgency)
- No emoji-stuffed captions
- No exclamation-stacking
- No "your one-stop destination for..." corporate filler
- No translated-from-English Arabic that feels stiff

## Pillars to lean on (rotate per ad)
1. **Local fresh** — Omani produce, Omani suppliers, named provenance
2. **Modern value** — premium feel at fair price, no shame in either
3. **Community** — daily rituals, family tables, neighbourhood moments
4. **National pride** — Vision 2040, Omani-grown, quietly
5. **Trust** — MAF muscle without foreign-brand feel

## Composition rules per ad
- Format: 4:5 vertical (1080x1350) for IG feed primary
- Hero subject takes 60–70% of frame, balanced not centred-locked
- Logo placement: small, top-left or bottom-right, never dominant
- Tagline: integrated, never floating overlay
- One message per ad — never mix value, community, and product hero in one frame
